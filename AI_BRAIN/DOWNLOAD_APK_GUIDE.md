# 📲 HOW TO DOWNLOAD & EXPORT YOUR APK (VISHU CONNECT)

### 1. Top-Right "Export" Menu (Direct Official Download):
Look at the top-right corner of your screen (right above the code editor):
1. Click on the **`Export ⌄`** button (or the `⚙️` Settings gear).
2. Select **"Download APK"** or **"Export as ZIP"**.
3. The APK will immediately download to your device!

---

### 2. Why .apk was hidden in the left File Explorer:
AI Studio's code editor is designed to view and edit text files (`.kt`, `.md`, `.json`). Binary files like `.apk` and `.jar` are built in the background and hidden from the text tree to prevent the editor from freezing.
